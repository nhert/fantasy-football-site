How to Play Pick'ems & Survivor Pool

Schedule and Deadlines

  • Each Tuesday when the clock hits Midnight Eastern, the next NFL Week is started and the previous weeks scores and eliminations will be processed
  • After the week starts, the deadline timer will start counting down to 10pm on the day of the first game that week
  • 12/14 weeks the deadline will be Thursday at 10pm Eastern
  • For 2026, Week 1 and Week 12 (American Thanksgiving) have their first game on a Wednesday, so the deadline will be Wednesday at 10pm Eastern
  • Pick'ems can be submitted for the current week and any future weeks
  • Survivor Pool will unlock on Tuesdays at Midnight Eastern when the week rotates forward. You may not make Survivor submissions for future weeks

Pick'ems

Registration

  • In order to register as a player in Pick'ems, all you need to do is make at least 1 pick
  • You may join after NFL Week 1, but you will receive 0 points for the weeks before you registered

How to Play

  • Each week, pick who will win every B3FL matchup and a poker chip will be placed to represent your choice
  • To remove a pick simply click on the poker chip and you may make a different selection
  • You can either:
    • Pick a GM (no penalty for incorrect pick)
    • Double Down and receive double the score (-2 penalty if incorrect)
    • Triple Down and receive triple the score (-3 penalty if incorrect)
  • Each player has only 1 Double Down and 1 Triple Down per week
  • Double and Triple Downs are optional and do not need to be used
  • In each Pick'ems matchup:
    • If a player has more fantasy wins than their opponent, they are considered the Favourite
    • If a player has less fantasy wins than their opponent, they are considered the Underdog
    • If both players have the same number of wins, it is considered Even
  • Choosing an Underdog grants bonus points, picking a Favourite or Even matchup grants normal points
  • While submitting for future weeks is allowed, Underdog status cannot be displayed past the current NFL week. You will still be given Underdog bonus points for correct picks once that week is processed

How to Win

  • At the end of week 14, the player with the most points wins the Pick'ems prize!

Scoring

  • 2 Points granted for correctly picking a Favourite or Even matchup
  • 3 Points granted for correctly picking an Underdog
  • 2x Points granted for correctly picking with a Double Down
  • 3x Points granted for correctly picking with a Triple Down
  • -2 Point Penalty for an incorrect Double Down
  • -3 Point Penalty for an incorrect Triple Down
  • If a matchup is a Tie, it's considered a push and 0 points will be rewarded and no penalties will be assigned

Missed Deadline

  • In Pick'ems, if you miss the submission deadline and you are registered (made at least 1 pick previously), you will be auto-assigned picks for matchups you did not pick
  • Auto-assigned picks are the same for every player
  • You will be auto-assigned the Underdog in every matchup and will receive NO bonus points for correct picks (2 points)
  • If a matchup is Even, the auto-assigned player will be chosen randomly

Survivor Pool

Registration

  • In order to register as a player in Survivor Pool, you must make a submission by the Week 1 deadline (Wednesday Sept 9 at 10pm Eastern)
  • You may NOT join Survivor Pool after the Week 1 deadline

How to Play

  • Each week, select one GM to win from the dropdown and hit submit. All players submissions can be seen displayed below
  • You may only choose each GM once per season
  • You may freely change your survivor selection up until the deadline of that week
  • If they win or tie, you survive. If they lose, you are ELIMINATED

How to Win

  • The player who survives the longest wins the Survivor Pool prize!
  • If multiple players are eliminated in the same week leaving no remaining survivors, all players from that week will tie for the prize
  • If week 14 ends and there are still multiple survivors, they tie
  • If players tie for the prize, they can elect to split the prize or add it onto next years prize pool

Missed Deadline

  • In Survivor Pool, if you miss the submission deadline you are ELIMINATED

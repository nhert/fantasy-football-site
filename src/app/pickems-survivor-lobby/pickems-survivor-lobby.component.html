How to Play Pick'ems & Survivor Pool

Schedule and Deadlines

  • The timer will count down to the first game that is played that NFL Week
  • Most weeks the deadline will be at the start of the Thursday game
  • For 2026, Week 1 and Week 12 (Thanksgiving) have their first game on a Wednesday, so pay attention!
  • The results for the week will be processed at 2am Wednesday EST (Tuesday Night - Wednesday Morning) so that enough time is allowed for stat corrections
  • After the week's results are processed, the next week will become available
  • Pick'ems can be submitted for the current week and any future weeks
  • Survivor Pool unlocks when the week rotates forward at 2am EST, and locks at that weeks deadline. You may not make future submissions.

Pick'ems

How to Play

  • In order to register as a player in Pick'ems, all you need to do is make at least 1 pick
  • You may join after NFL Week 1, but you will receive 0 points for the weeks before you registered
  • Each week, pick who will win every B3FL matchup
  • To remove a pick simply click on the "Pick" poker chip and you may make a different selection before the deadline
  • You can either Pick a GM, Double Down and receive double the score, or Triple Down and receive triple the score
  • Each player has only 1 Double Down and 1 Triple Down per week
  • Double and Triple Downs are completely optional and do not need to be used each week
  • If a player has more fantasy wins than their opponent, they are considered the Favourite and their opponent is considered the Underdog
  • If both players have the same number of wins, it is considered an Even matchup
  • Choosing an Underdog grants bonus points, picking a Favourite or Even matchup grants normal points
  • Submitting for future weeks is allowed, but Underdog status cannot be displayed past the current NFL week. You will still be given Underdog bonus points for correct picks once that week is processed.

How to Win

  • At the end of the season, the player with the most points wins the Pick'ems prize!

Scoring

  • 2 Points granted for correctly picking a Favourite or Even matchup
  • 3 Points granted for correctly picking an Underdog
  • 2x Points granted for correctly picking with a Double Down
  • 3x Points granted for correctly picking with a Triple Down
  • -2 Point Penalty applied for missing on a Double Down
  • -3 Point Penalty applied for missing on a Triple Down
  • If a matchup is a Tie, it's considered a push and 0 points will be rewarded and no penalties will be assigned

Missed Deadline

  • In Pick'ems, if you miss the submission deadline and you are registered (made at least 1 pick in the past), you will be auto-assigned picks for matchups you did not pick
  • Auto-assigned picks are the same for every player
  • You will be auto-assigned the Underdog in every matchup and will receive NO bonus points for correct picks (2 points)
  • If a matchup is Even, the auto-assigned player will be chosen randomly

Survivor Pool

How to Play

  • In order to register as a player in Survivor Pool, you must make a submission by the Week 1 deadline (first game of Week 1)
  • You may NOT join Survivor Pool after the Week 1 deadline
  • Each week, select one GM to win that week. You may only choose each GM once per season
  • If they win or tie, you survive. If they lose, you are ELIMINATED

How to Win

  • The player who survives the longest wins the Survivor Pool prize!
  • If multiple players are eliminated in the same week leaving no remaining survivors, all players from that week will tie for the prize
  • If players tie for the prize, they can elect to split the prize or add it onto next years prize pool

Missed Deadline

  • In Survivor Pool, if you miss the submission deadline you are ELIMINATED
